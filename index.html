02 The Forge

Four stages. One continuous heat.

  1. 01

    Idea — raw ore

    We pressure-test the idea: who it’s for, what it must do, and the smallest version worth shipping. You leave with a fixed scope and a fixed price.

  2. 02

    Design — the mould

    Flows, wireframes, then high-fidelity UI you can click through. The shape is set before a line of production code is poured.

  3. 03

    Build — the strike

    One-week sprints, a working demo every Friday. Tests, CI and code review from the first commit — not bolted on at the end.

  4. 04

    Launch — the quench

    Web, App Store, Google Play. Monitoring in place, then a clean handover: your repo, your cloud, your keys.
