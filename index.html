SCROLL TO DIVE
01 SUNLIT ZONE

Below 200 meters, sunlight can no longer sustain photosynthesis. You are leaving the base of Earth's food chain behind, on purpose.

02 TWILIGHT ZONE

Ninety percent of the ocean's biomass drifts here, in near-total darkness. More life than anywhere else on Earth — and almost none of it has ever seen the sun.

03 MIDNIGHT ZONE

No sunlight has touched this water in millions of years. Every spark you see out there is a living thing making its own light — the only light for miles.

04 THE FLOOR

Hydrothermal vents here scald at 400°C — yet the water around them never boils. At this pressure, three hundred and eighty atmospheres, it simply can't.
